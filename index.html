Mike Dzikowski

I build the security controls that go underneath other people’s cloud infrastructure — landing zones, hardened images, sensor deployment at fleet scale. Currently at CrowdStrike. Ten years at Microsoft before that.

Most of my work is infrastructure-as-code for environments where the compliance boundary is the hard part: DoD Secure Cloud Computing Architecture, Zero Trust reference designs, and the unglamorous plumbing that makes a control actually enforceable across thousands of machines.

It is nearly all public. 88 repositories, mostly Bicep and PowerShell, and 31 merged pull requests into Microsoft’s Mission Landing Zone.
